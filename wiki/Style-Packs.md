# Style Packs

The same studio workflow is used for every style pack. A style pack changes the instructions used to interpret the images and create the final image; it does not change the review and approval step.

## Architectural photography

Use for interiors and spatial visualization. The prompt pack prioritizes preserving the supplied layout, proportions, spatial arrangement, and architectural elements. References should direct materials, finish level, lighting, and atmosphere rather than request structural changes.

Best example: `ref_01/kitchen`.

## Food photography

Use for menu, advertising, and social-ready food images. The prompt pack prioritizes preserving the dish, plating layout, camera composition, and core ingredients. References should direct lighting, color grading, texture realism, and presentation polish.

## Jewellery photography

Use for jewellery-on-model and jewellery product work. The prompt pack prioritizes exact jewellery geometry, recognizable model identity, camera framing, and natural material and skin rendering. References should direct the finish, styling, lighting, packaging, or presentation, but must not ask to redesign the product.

Best examples: `ref_01/arnav`, `ref_01/Lavanya`, `ref_01/mansi`, `ref_01/rat`, `ref_01/She`, and `ref_01/taruni_jewel`.

## Choosing the right pack

Select the pack based on the base image's subject, then phrase references as constraints. Do not select a pack merely for a color palette or overall mood. The selected pack controls what the system treats as a non-negotiable part of the anchor image.
