# AI Photo Studio

AI Photo Studio helps a creator turn an existing product or spatial reference into a polished commercial image without losing the thing that makes the input valuable: its jewellery details, model identity, dish identity, or room geometry.

The studio uses a two-stage workflow. First, it interprets the anchor image, supporting references, selected style, and written direction into an editable scene description. After the user approves that description, it generates one final image.

## The opportunity

Many teams have useful source images that are not ready for a campaign: a phone photo of jewellery, an unfinished interior visualization, or an everyday food image. This tool gives them a structured path from source asset to a presentation-ready image while keeping a human reviewer in control of the brief.

## What the MVP does not claim

The generated result is a creative output that should be reviewed before publishing. The tool aims to retain key details and composition according to its prompt packs, but it does not replace product approval, brand review, or source-of-truth product photography.
